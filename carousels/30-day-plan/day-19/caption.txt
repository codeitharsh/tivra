The 4-step framework to finally finish side projects:

1. Scope it down until it's almost embarrassing.
2. Ship an ugly version in week one.
3. Set a hard deadline, not a vague one.
4. Tell one person who'll check.

Most side projects don't die from lack of skill. They die from lack of scope.
